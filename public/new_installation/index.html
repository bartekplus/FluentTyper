Shortcuts for things you often write

Create a shortcut for a phrase, an email reply, or your signature. FluentTyper expands it as you type. Add your shortcuts in Settings → Snippets & Dictionary.

Language and suggestions

Choose your writing language and whether suggestions appear in a popup or in your text. In Settings → Grammar, choose which corrections run while you type and which checks run in Review. You can also turn on advice about style and readability.

More suggestions with local AI

If your device supports it, you can add AI suggestions to Review in Chrome and Edge. Turn on Local AI Review in Settings → Grammar. With your permission, the browser downloads a model; this needs an internet connection. After that, it checks text on your device. Autocomplete and standard Review work without it. Local AI Review isn’t available in Firefox.

Your text stays in your browser

Word suggestions and standard Review work offline. There’s no account to create, and FluentTyper doesn’t collect usage data. Website access lets it work in supported text fields. Password fields and other sensitive fields are excluded.

Not seeing suggestions?

Check website access above and reload the tab if it was already open. Try a regular text field. If the site has its own suggestions, click the icon in the corner of the field. You can also open the extension popup to check whether FluentTyper is paused for that site.
