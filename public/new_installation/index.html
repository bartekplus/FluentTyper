What happens after access

The goal is first success, not a tour. These are the only setup details most people need before FluentTyper becomes useful.

Why this permission exists

FluentTyper needs website access so the content script can watch text fields and render suggestions where you type.

What stays private

Suggestions are generated on-device. Your typed content is not uploaded, tracked, or sent to a remote service.

If you do not see suggestions

Reload an already open tab, click into a regular text field, and type a short phrase with a trailing space so the predictor has context.

What FluentTyper adds after setup

Once the first suggestion works, these are the capabilities you can explore next.

Autocomplete

Predicts the next word or phrase while you type, directly inside supported text inputs.

Text expansions

Expands shortcuts into longer phrases, templates, and repeated snippets.

Offline-first behavior

Core prediction stays available even without a network connection, so the extension is useful anywhere.
